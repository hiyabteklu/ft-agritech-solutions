Where It All Began

Vision

To make Ethiopia the world's leading example of technology-driven agriculture by innovating practical solutions to every major agricultural challenge.

Mission

FT Agri-Tech exists to identify real agricultural challenges through research, field engagement, and data, then design, build, deploy, and continuously improve engineering solutions that create measurable value for farmers, businesses, and institutions.

Why We Exist

FT Agri-Tech was born from lived experience. We grew up watching hardworking farmers lose harvests to pests, wildlife, unpredictable weather, disease, and problems that often had practical solutions but no one built them.

Philosophy

We don't invent technology and search for a use.
We find the problem first, then build exactly what the situation demands.
